# The Flow of Durable Legal Knowledge

OpenLaws follows one calm conviction: **the value is not in the prompt.**

## `Sources → Knowledge → Skills → Application`

1. **Sources**: Authoritative statutes, case law and internal positions remain anchored to their verifiable origin.
2. **Knowledge**: Context and provenance are preserved in a living structure that both humans and machines can read and audit.
3. **Skills**: Repeatable legal judgment, boundaries and escalation rules are distilled into enduring craft.
4. **Application**: Models may change from quarter to quarter. Your legal standard remains sovereign and consistent.

*Selected specifications and building blocks will be shared on [github.com/openlaws-org](https://github.com/openlaws-org) step by step when their time has come.*
